Acrabond 973: The Practical Solution for General Use

Acrabond 973 is generally preferred for applications where flexibility and ease of use matter more than extreme performance. It is a highly reliable choice for routine industrial tasks.

Best Use Cases for 973:

  • Standard packaging-related work.
  • General assembly applications.
  • Situations with standard room temperature conditions.

Acrabond 978: Engineered for Demanding Conditions

If you are looking for high-performance industrial bonding solutions, Acrabond 978 is designed for environments where failure is not an option. It is specifically built to handle stress, load, and higher temperatures common in heavy machinery sectors.

Best Use Cases for 978:

  • Heavy machinery bonding.
  • High-temperature industrial environments.
  • Structural or load-bearing applications.
